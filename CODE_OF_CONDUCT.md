# Code of Conduct

Tapvio is a small project. Be direct, be kind, and keep the conversation on the work.

- Treat people with respect. No insults, harassment, or sexual content.
- Disagree with the change, not the person who wrote it.
- Pages added here follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md): one page per person or brand, no impersonation, and no spam or illegal content.
- Maintainers may edit or remove issues, comments, and profiles that break these rules.

To report a conduct problem, contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile: https://github.com/mafzalkalwardev
