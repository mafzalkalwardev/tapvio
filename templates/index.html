Built for being found

Most link pages are built for social apps and ignored by search. A Tapvio page is a real web page, so your name, work and profiles can appear in Google.

Indexed by search engines

Plain HTML, a sitemap and structured data that tells Google all your profiles belong to you. New pages are announced to Bing within minutes.

Nine real themes

From a CV layout to a Windows 95 window. Each theme has its own fonts and layout, with light and dark modes where they make sense.

Save contact button

Add an email or phone number and visitors can save you to their phone contacts in one tap. A share image is made for every page.

Yours to keep

Your page is one small text file in a public repository. No lock-in: copy it, host it yourself, or move it to your own domain.

How it works

Three steps, about five minutes. You need a free GitHub account to publish.

  1. Fill in the builder

    Add your name, a short bio and your links. Pick a theme and watch the preview update as you type.

  2. Publish on GitHub

    One button opens GitHub with your page file ready. Click Propose changes, then Create pull request.

  3. Go live

    Once it's approved, your page is live at {{ cfg.site_url.split('://')[1] }}/yourname and search engines are told about it.
