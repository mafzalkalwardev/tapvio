Tapvio vs Linktree

Tapvio is a free, open-source Linktree alternative. Both give you one short address that lists your website, social profiles and contact details. They are built very differently, and each suits a different person. This page sets out the differences as plainly as we can. Linktree details come from its own site; check Linktree's pricing page for what it currently offers.

The short answer

  • Pick Tapvio if you want a free link-in-bio page that is plain, fast HTML, that search engines can read, that lives in a file you own, and that you can copy or self-host.
  • Pick Linktree if you want an account you sign into with an email address, a hosted editor and phone app, and features Tapvio does not have, such as click analytics or selling through your page (on Linktree's paid plans).

Why Tapvio is a good Linktree alternative

Most link-in-bio tools are built for social apps, and the page they give you can be heavy on scripts. A Tapvio link-in-bio page is server-rendered HTML with no JavaScript framework. Published pages are not marked noindex. Each one has its own title, description, canonical URL and structured data, an automatic share image for LinkedIn, X, WhatsApp and Slack, and an entry in the sitemap. That gives search engines what they need to find your name and the profiles you own. Whether and when a page ranks is still up to the search engine.

Your page is one file. You can copy it, fork the project, or run your own copy on your own domain. Nothing is locked inside an account.

What Tapvio does not do

  • No click analytics, because Tapvio does not track visitors.
  • No selling, payments or email collection from the page.
  • Changes are not instant. They go live after your pull request is checked and merged.
  • You need a free GitHub account to publish.

If any of these matter to you, Linktree is likely the better tool.

Questions

Is Tapvio a free Linktree alternative?

Yes. Tapvio is free and open source under the MIT licence, with no paid plans, ads or tracking. Publishing needs a free GitHub account.

Can Google index a Tapvio link-in-bio page?

Published pages are static HTML without a noindex tag, with a unique title, description, canonical URL, JSON-LD and a sitemap entry. Indexing is still the search engine's decision.

Can I move from Linktree to Tapvio?

Yes, by hand. Open the builder, add the same links, pick a theme and publish. Then put your new Tapvio address in your social bios. There is no automatic import.
